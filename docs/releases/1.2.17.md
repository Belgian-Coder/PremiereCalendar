# v1.2.17 — responsive cached calendars

A complete fresh calendar cache becomes ready after local hydration. Optional
ratings, artwork and availability repairs continue on the existing durable job
and Blazor SignalR connection. The page shows “Calendar ready · Updating details”
and accepts navigation while those repairs run. Final job completion, persistence,
source checkpoints and recovery retain their terminal meaning.

Accepted enrichment is published in bounded batches instead of waiting behind
two whole-week enrichment barriers. Each update reapplies the active filters,
keeps card order, and removes cards that no longer qualify. Unprocessed cards keep
their locally hydrated values. Provider limits and result coverage are preserved.
No new SignalR hub or SSE connection is introduced.

Cold or expired discovery still reports loading until its coverage is known.
Calendar readiness means the cached calendar is available; metadata validation
can still trigger discovery changes. This release does not claim a cold-download
or total provider-completion speedup.

OMDb diagnostics distinguish known invalid-ID, provider-data, missing-error,
quota and authentication responses without logging arbitrary provider text,
titles, URLs or credentials. Retry and good-data preservation behavior is unchanged.
The exact error messages behind the previous 24 failures were not retained and
cannot be reconstructed from the earlier traces.

Validation and installed acceptance will be recorded in the local release
manifest. Builds, tests, security scans, SBOM generation and publication run
locally; GitHub workflows stay disabled. PostgreSQL, saved settings, application
data and backups are preserved.
